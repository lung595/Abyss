Abyss - wake a sleeping device (NAK-271)

One idea: a sleeping device is an unlit creature resting on the floor. Touch it, light rises through its body, and it leaves the floor if it answers. Fictitious data. Real sizes (view 580 × 480, card 360, settings 366 and 560).

Theme Option Reduce motion Notes in

1 · The view: unlit creature, wake, result

Click a creature on the floor, or use the buttons. The real wait is 20 s (3 s here).

Left: known MAC, a thin ring opens on hover and keyboard focus, the sub label becomes "Wake". Right: unknown MAC, no ring, the click explains why (never a silent refusal).

States

A · Rising tide [recommended]: light rises in the body like a water level (300 ms), holds during the wait, then the creature leaves the floor, or the light drains (200 ms). Not generic: the animal's own body is the gauge, no bar, no spinner.

B · Lantern spark: a spark climbs the body and opens a halo. Closer to the whale's lantern, but failure reads less clearly (the halo only fades).

Reduce motion: no movement, the body lights at once and the text says the state. Nothing spins during the wait: a 2 px line steps once per second (1 Hz Timer, only while the view is open).

2 · The peer card

One primary action per card. The state is said by an icon and a text, never by colour alone.

3 · Notes (value 10: guided, never blocked)

Short title, one sentence saying what to do, GitHub mark (44 px target) to docs/GUIDE.md#wake-a-device. Copy from the NAK-270 engine, unchanged.

4 · Settings: wake light and learned addresses

Narrow form (366 px, with the rail) and wide (560 px). The title wraps before the switch, buttons drop under each other, nothing overlaps.

5 · Contrast measured in this page (current theme)

WCAG ratio on the colours actually applied (backgrounds composited). Text ≥ 4.5, UI parts ≥ 3. The unlit body (38 %) is decorative: the name and sub label carry the information.

6 · Cost to build

A: a second drawing of the creature in a clipped Item, two 300 ms translations, then nothing (0 at rest); about half a day more than B because of the Canvas clip. B: one circle and one halo, cheapest. Both: a 1 Hz Timer during the 20 s wait, stopped when the view closes; battery cost to be measured by the Performance Engineer in NAK-224 (not measured here).

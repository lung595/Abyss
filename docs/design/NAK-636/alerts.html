Abyss · Favourite alerts (NAK-636, for NAK-229)

Idea: a favourite surfaces or sinks. The jellyfish of the toast is lit and full when the device is back, hollow and sunk when it is offline. In the settings, a line with gaps shows which drops stay silent for the wait you picked.

Why it is not generic: the state is carried by the plugin's own creature and by the shape of the outage (a gap in a line), not by a red or green dot.

Behaviour

Grouping: every event returned by one Alerts.step call makes at most two toasts, one for the "down" events and one for the "back" events. The sentence for 2 and for 3 or more is new pure logic for Alerts.js (with gjs tests). A muted favourite stays quiet, like today.

Motion: none at rest. The toast entrance is DMS's own. The toggle knob slides 22 px in 150 ms (existing row); with Reduce motion everything changes at once.

Narrow form (440 px with the 176 px rail, 216 px left for the rows): titles wrap before the switch, the delay pills wrap onto a second line, the pill reason drops under the chip. Nothing overlaps (checked by script on this page, both widths).

Open point, not verified in a build: ToastService.showInfo has no icon parameter (message, details, command, category). With a plain toast the shell draws its own info icon ("Toast icon" switch above). The jellyfish needs either a desktop notification with the plugin icon or a small Abyss-owned toast; that choice belongs to NAK-229.

The "Light on battery" level rests on a written reason (the alert step runs inside the refresh Abyss already does, no extra process or timer). It is not measured yet: to be confirmed by the bench of NAK-229.

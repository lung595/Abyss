Usage: abyss [command]

  abyss                          open Abyss
  abyss send <device> [files…]   send files to a device
  abyss peer <device>            open a device
  abyss map                      open the map
  abyss settings [category]      open the settings
  abyss --help | --version

A second `abyss` raises the window already open instead of starting another.
Guide: https://github.com/lung595/Abyss/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md#app-command-line
